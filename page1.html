ABOUT US

Founded in 2002, Lex & Partners Law Firm has built a strong reputation for trust, transparency, and
outstanding legal service.

Over the past two decades, we have represented clients across Malaysia and internationally, offering
solutions that balance legal precision with practical results.

Our approach is simple:
We listen carefully, analyze deeply, and act decisively.

With a diverse team of legal professionals, we specialize in both corporate and personal legal matters
— ensuring that each case receives personalized attention and care.

Our Mission
To provide professional, ethical, and effective legal services that uphold justice and serve the best
interests of our clients.

Our Vision
To be a trusted law firm recognized for integrity, innovation, and excellence in legal practice.

PRACTICE AREAS

    At Lex &amp; Partners, our expertise spans across multiple legal disciplines:

    1. Corporate &amp; Commercial Law

    We assist companies in drafting, reviewing, and negotiating commercial agreements, mergers,
    acquisitions, and compliance issues.

    Our goal is to protect your business interests while ensuring long-term growth.

    2. Civil &amp; Criminal Litigation

    Our litigation team represents clients in civil disputes, contract breaches, personal injury claims, and
    criminal defense.

    We focus on strong case strategy, thorough research, and persuasive argumentation.

    3. Real Estate &amp; Property Law

    From property transactions to tenancy disputes, our firm provides reliable legal support to buyers,
    sellers, and developers.

    4. Family &amp; Divorce Law

    We handle sensitive family matters with care, confidentiality, and respect — including divorce, child
    custody, and asset division.

    5. Employment Law

    We guide both employers and employees through workplace disputes, unfair dismissals, and contractual
    issues.

    6. Intellectual Property (IP) Law

    Our IP team protects your creative assets — trademarks, copyrights, and patents — from infringement
    and misuse.
